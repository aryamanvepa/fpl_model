FPL Multi-Model Bot

Four different ways to decide a Fantasy Premier League squad, run against each other, combined, and made fully inspectable.

Four genuinely different decision-making approaches — a hand-tuned formula, a trained statistical model, an evolved strategy, and an LLM reading the news — each running its own live squad, combined by a weighted ensemble, with a dashboard exposing the actual internals of every model rather than just a final recommendation.

A static snapshot — charts stay interactive, but data only refreshes when the models are re-run locally.

ModelApproachResult
1 — Evolutionary Genetic algorithm — a population of strategies plays simulated seasons; the best survive and breed +101 pts vs. a non-evolved baseline, out-of-training
2 — Statistical Gradient-boosted trees on 3 seasons of real gameweek data MAE 1.02 vs. 1.08 naive, out-of-training
3 — Basic stats Transparent hand-weighted formula The baseline the others are measured against
4 — Qualitative LLM reads news + injury status for a shortlist of players Single-pass synthesis, not a tool-using agent

Python throughout — PuLP for the squad optimizer, scikit-learn for the predictor, a from-scratch genetic algorithm, Streamlit + Plotly for the live dashboard. Data from the official FPL API and the public vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League historical dataset.
